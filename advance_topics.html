Advanced Topics in Women's Studies

Queer Theory and Transgender Studies

The critical deconstruction of normative sexuality, binary gender constructs, and institutional heteronormativity through queer, trans, and intersectional theoretical frameworks.

Queer Theory: An Introduction (Annamarie Jagose) - The primary introductory textbook for this subject, tracing the trajectory of queer theoretical concepts from early gay and lesbian liberation through poststructuralist critique.

Yale OpenCourses ENGL 300 - Lecture 23: Queer Theory and Gender Performativity (Paul Fry / Yale University) - A concise open video lecture that complements Jagose's survey by closely examining Judith Butler's gender performativity in dialogue with Michel Foucault's history of sexuality.

Transgender History: The Roots of Today's Revolution (Susan Stryker) - A foundational historical narrative providing historical grounding in twentieth-century transgender activism, bodily autonomy, and evolving conceptual categories.

Disidentifications: Queers of Color and the Performance of Politics (José Esteban Muñoz) - Builds upon foundational performativity theory by examining how racialized and queer subjects negotiate, resist, and transform dominant cultural ideologies.

Queer Cinema and Visual Culture (MIT OpenCourseWare) - An applied undergraduate course syllabus applying queer analytical frameworks to twentieth- and twenty-first-century film, censorship regimes, and visual representation.

Feminist Science, Technology, and Environmental Studies

How gender, race, and power relations intersect with scientific knowledge construction, technological design, and ecological systems.

Science Activism: Gender, Race, and Power (MIT OpenCourseWare) - A complete university course framework investigating the politics of scientific institutions, environmental justice struggles, and the social responsibility of scientists.

Simians, Cyborgs, and Women: The Reinvention of Nature (Donna Haraway) - The canonical text featuring "A Cyborg Manifesto" and "Situated Knowledges," challenging biological determinism and articulating how objectivity requires partial, accountable perspectives.

Ecofeminism: Women, Animals, Nature (Greta Gaard, ed.) - A freely accessible anthology articulating the philosophical and political connections between patriarchal domination, animal exploitation, and ecological degradation.

Meeting the Universe Halfway: Quantum Physics and the Entanglement of Matter and Meaning (Karen Barad) - An advanced theoretical work extending Haraway's insights into "agential realism," integrating quantum physics with feminist theory to rethink matter, performativity, and ethics.

Feminist Epistemologies and Knowledge Production

The philosophical examination of how gender, race, and colonial hierarchies shape standards of evidence, credibility, and what counts as authoritative knowledge.

Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing (Miranda Fricker) - The foundational philosophical text introducing testimonial and hermeneutical injustice, analyzing how prejudice systematically deflates a speaker's credibility or obscures social experience.

Data Feminism (Catherine D'Ignazio and Lauren F. Klein / MIT Press) - An open-access book applying intersectional feminist epistemology to modern data science, setting out concrete principles for auditing power, binaries, and invisible labor in digital systems.

Decolonizing Methodologies: Research and Indigenous Peoples (Linda Tuhiwai Smith) - A seminal critique of Western imperialist knowledge production, establishing frameworks for respectful, ethical inquiry centered on Indigenous sovereignty and standpoint.

Feminist Political Economy and Social Reproduction

The gendered dimensions of labor, global capitalism, unpaid domestic provisioning, and the legal structures governing human care.

Gender, Development, and Globalization: Economics as if All People Mattered (Lourdes Benería, Günseli Berik, and Maria Floro) - The core survey textbook for this subject, methodically dismantling neoclassical economic assumptions by examining unpaid reproductive labor, care provisioning, and macroeconomic development.

Caliban and the Witch: Women, the Body and Primitive Accumulation (Silvia Federici, Internet Archive) - A historical and theoretical primary text establishing how early capitalist development structurally depended on the enclosure of the commons and the subjugation of women's reproductive labor.

Social Reproduction Theory: Remapping Class, Recentering Oppression (Tithi Bhattacharya, ed.) - An essential collection of contemporary theoretical essays analyzing how the daily replenishment and generational replacement of human labor power sustains the capitalist system.

Global Woman: Nannies, Maids, and Sex Workers in the New Economy (Barbara Ehrenreich and Arlie Russell Hochschild, eds.) - An empirical anthology investigating transnational care chains, illustrating how the extraction of emotional and domestic labor connects women in the Global South with households in the Global North.
