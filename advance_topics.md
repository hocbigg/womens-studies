# Advanced Topics in Women's Studies

## Queer Theory and Transgender Studies

The critical deconstruction of normative sexuality, binary gender constructs, and institutional heteronormativity through queer, trans, and intersectional theoretical frameworks.

[Queer Theory: An Introduction (Annamarie Jagose)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780814742341) - The primary introductory textbook for this subject, tracing the trajectory of queer theoretical concepts from early gay and lesbian liberation through poststructuralist critique.

[Yale OpenCourses ENGL 300 - Lecture 23: Queer Theory and Gender Performativity (Paul Fry / Yale University)](https://oyc.yale.edu/english/engl-300/lecture-23) - A concise open video lecture that complements Jagose's survey by closely examining Judith Butler's gender performativity in dialogue with Michel Foucault's history of sexuality.

[Transgender History: The Roots of Today's Revolution (Susan Stryker)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781580056892) - A foundational historical narrative providing historical grounding in twentieth-century transgender activism, bodily autonomy, and evolving conceptual categories.

[Disidentifications: Queers of Color and the Performance of Politics (José Esteban Muñoz)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780816630554) - Builds upon foundational performativity theory by examining how racialized and queer subjects negotiate, resist, and transform dominant cultural ideologies.

[Queer Cinema and Visual Culture (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-181-queer-cinema-and-visual-culture-fall-2017/) - An applied undergraduate course syllabus applying queer analytical frameworks to twentieth- and twenty-first-century film, censorship regimes, and visual representation.

## Feminist Science, Technology, and Environmental Studies

How gender, race, and power relations intersect with scientific knowledge construction, technological design, and ecological systems.

[Science Activism: Gender, Race, and Power (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-160j-science-activism-gender-race-and-power-fall-2019/) - A complete university course framework investigating the politics of scientific institutions, environmental justice struggles, and the social responsibility of scientists.

[Simians, Cyborgs, and Women: The Reinvention of Nature (Donna Haraway)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415904841) - The canonical text featuring "A Cyborg Manifesto" and "Situated Knowledges," challenging biological determinism and articulating how objectivity requires partial, accountable perspectives.

[Ecofeminism: Women, Animals, Nature (Greta Gaard, ed.)](https://archive.org/details/ecofeminismwomen0000unse) - A freely accessible anthology articulating the philosophical and political connections between patriarchal domination, animal exploitation, and ecological degradation.

[Meeting the Universe Halfway: Quantum Physics and the Entanglement of Matter and Meaning (Karen Barad)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780822339632) - An advanced theoretical work extending Haraway's insights into "agential realism," integrating quantum physics with feminist theory to rethink matter, performativity, and ethics.

## Feminist Epistemologies and Knowledge Production

The philosophical examination of how gender, race, and colonial hierarchies shape standards of evidence, credibility, and what counts as authoritative knowledge.

[Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing (Miranda Fricker)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198237907) - The foundational philosophical text introducing testimonial and hermeneutical injustice, analyzing how prejudice systematically deflates a speaker's credibility or obscures social experience.

[Data Feminism (Catherine D'Ignazio and Lauren F. Klein / MIT Press)](https://data-feminism.mitpress.mit.edu/) - An open-access book applying intersectional feminist epistemology to modern data science, setting out concrete principles for auditing power, binaries, and invisible labor in digital systems.

[Decolonizing Methodologies: Research and Indigenous Peoples (Linda Tuhiwai Smith)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781783604524) - A seminal critique of Western imperialist knowledge production, establishing frameworks for respectful, ethical inquiry centered on Indigenous sovereignty and standpoint.

## Feminist Political Economy and Social Reproduction

The gendered dimensions of labor, global capitalism, unpaid domestic provisioning, and the legal structures governing human care.

[Gender, Development, and Globalization: Economics as if All People Mattered (Lourdes Benería, Günseli Berik, and Maria Floro)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415537490) - The core survey textbook for this subject, methodically dismantling neoclassical economic assumptions by examining unpaid reproductive labor, care provisioning, and macroeconomic development.

[Caliban and the Witch: Women, the Body and Primitive Accumulation (Silvia Federici, Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/calibanwitchwome0000fede) - A historical and theoretical primary text establishing how early capitalist development structurally depended on the enclosure of the commons and the subjugation of women's reproductive labor.

[Social Reproduction Theory: Remapping Class, Recentering Oppression (Tithi Bhattacharya, ed.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780745339061) - An essential collection of contemporary theoretical essays analyzing how the daily replenishment and generational replacement of human labor power sustains the capitalist system.

[Global Woman: Nannies, Maids, and Sex Workers in the New Economy (Barbara Ehrenreich and Arlie Russell Hochschild, eds.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780805075090) - An empirical anthology investigating transnational care chains, illustrating how the extraction of emotional and domestic labor connects women in the Global South with households in the Global North.
